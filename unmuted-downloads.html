A structured reflection worksheet for participants and facilitators

Why This Worksheet Exists

Mental health stigma persists partly because people who have lived it are rarely given the structured, unhurried space to name their own experience before being asked to share it.

"The worksheet is a reflection tool, not a disclosure tool."

Unmuted is designed to help participants move from lived experience toward meaning-making, context, audience awareness, and intentional impact. Each prompt is deliberately sequenced. No one is asked to share unless they choose to.

The 60-Minute Arc

Welcome + Grounding (10 min)

Establish consent. Lead a three-breath grounding. Set the tone: slow, quiet, unhurried.

Five Guided Prompts (35 min)

Silent writing on: story carried, lessons learned, identity + context, audience, impact.

Framework Walk-Through (6 min)

Introduce the Unmuted Narrative Framework (A–E) as optional scaffolding for future sharing.

Closing Reflection (9 min)

"Your story is not just personal — it is a catalyst." Aftercare resources shared aloud.
